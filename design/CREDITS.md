# Logo attribution

The technology and social logo paths in `icons/` are from [Simple Icons 13.21.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/13.21.0), distributed under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/13.21.0/LICENSE.md).

The paths are reproduced in monochrome. Names and logos remain the property of their respective owners; their inclusion identifies tools used and profile destinations.

Public contribution data is fetched from the owner's [GitHub contribution calendar](https://github.com/users/shryssssss-maker/contributions). `activity.json` records its fetch date. Values are GitHub's actual activity intensity levels, not invented counts.
